## Overview

RASHT-3D is a research direction for three-dimensional Network-on-Chip design. The current baseline targets a 16×16 organization containing 256 processing elements and adds vertical communication links to explore three-dimensional connectivity.

## Design objectives

- Study the effect of vertical links on communication paths and network organization.
- Evaluate latency, energy, and area as a joint design trade-off.
- Maintain a consistent precision and workload setup when comparing architectural alternatives.
- Develop the model in C++ before progressing to RTL and ModelSim-oriented verification.

## Evaluation principles

Comparisons should use identical traffic traces, injection rates, packet sizes, routing assumptions, and reporting conventions. Results will be reported only after the corresponding simulations or synthesis runs have been completed and checked.
