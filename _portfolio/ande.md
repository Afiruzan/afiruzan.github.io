## Overview

Adaptive Network Differential Equations (ANDE) is a research framework investigating continuous and stochastic optimization ideas for discrete hardware-mapping problems, particularly processing-element placement and interconnect configuration.

## Research components

The proposed mathematical direction explores:

- Continuous placement representations and gradient-flow dynamics.
- Reaction–diffusion-inspired mechanisms for exploring bypass and connectivity patterns.
- Traffic propagation models motivated by heat-equation dynamics.
- Matrix normalization and assignment constraints, including Sinkhorn-style methods.
- Stochastic exploration and convergence analysis.

## Validation requirements

The framework's theoretical properties and practical utility must be established separately. Claims about convergence, approximation quality, runtime, or superiority require formal assumptions, proofs where applicable, and reproducible comparisons against established placement and mapping baselines.
