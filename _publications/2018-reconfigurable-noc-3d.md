### Summary

This work studies a reconfigurable Network-on-Chip for three-dimensional neural-network accelerators. It focuses on the on-chip communication fabric that connects accelerator resources in a 3D organization, where configurable interconnect structure can help accommodate communication requirements across the architecture. The paper appeared at the 2018 IEEE/ACM International Symposium on Networks-on-Chip (NOCS).

**Authors:** Arash Firuzan, Mehdi Modarressi, Masoud Daneshtalab, and Midia Reshadi.
