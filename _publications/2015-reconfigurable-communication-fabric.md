### Summary

The paper addresses communication among hardware resources used to implement neural networks. It presents a reconfigurable communication fabric intended to adapt interconnection resources to neural-network implementation requirements, rather than relying on a single fixed communication structure. The work is positioned in the context of reconfigurable communication-centric systems-on-chip.

**Authors:** Arash Firuzan, Mehdi Modarressi, and Masoud Daneshtalab.
